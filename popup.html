Claim Auto-Fill

Review before applying approved amounts

How it works:

  • Extension automatically detects claim and approved amount fields
  • Fills empty approved amounts with corresponding claim amounts
  • Audit checks bundled packages billed together with their components
  • Flagged rows get an [RGHS-AUDIT] remark and colour highlight
  • Audit flags are screening aids, not automatic rejections — verify documentation
  • Preview changes before applying; undo while the page remains open
