Claim Spark

Privacy

Claim Spark processes RGHS claim content locally in the authorized processor's browser. The Firebase service receives account, organization, role, licence, invitation, rate-limit and privacy-safe event metadata. It does not receive patient names, claim identifiers, clinical text, claim amounts, portal URLs or remarks during routine operation.

Individual payments

Individual users pay outside the extension using the official UPI QR code supplied by an administrator. Claim Spark stores the submitted UPI transaction ID (UTR), payment-verification status, verifier and verification time for access administration and audit purposes. It does not request or store UPI PINs, OTPs, card numbers, passwords or bank-account credentials.

The payment reference remains with the account record until it is replaced or the account is deleted and redacted under the account-deletion process. For privacy requests, use the support page.
